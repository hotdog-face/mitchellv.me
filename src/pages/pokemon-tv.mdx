<h3 style={{ color: '#43c296' }}>Problem</h3>

In March of 2021, young Pokémon fans could exclusively access Pokémon TV (PTV) via web; the streaming service needed to be ported to the Nintendo Switch™ to increase accessibility for its most important audience. TPCI created this streaming service with the intent of influencing sales of Pokémon Trading Cards - making the bold choice to exclude monetization. The streaming service needed to be easy for young viewers to navigate while offering a vast library of Pokémon’s entertainment content.

<h3 style={{ color: '#43c296' }}>Goals</h3>

- Influence sales of Pokémon Trading Cards
- Port PTV to Nintendo Switch™
- Drive new Pokémon viewers to the platform to achieve 7.2M average monthly USA views in 2021
- Expand the total reach of Pokémon’s home entertainment
- Increase customer lifetime value (LTV) while growing brand loyalty
- Create a polished experience that is fun and sparks user delight

</Intro>

<Outcomes stat="+33.5%" label="Monthly active users" left="#fafafa" right="#00785a" height={671}>
Upon its launch in August 2021, Pokémon TV for Nintendo Switch™ was promoted on the official Pokémon YouTube channel through a [launch announcement video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eefmgiP7Kfo). Following its launch, the **monthly active users across all platforms increased by 33.5%**. On average, users watched for 1 hour, with series and movies being the most engaging content, followed by junior's content. The app was developed to support new releases of Pokémon content and has continued to serve customers in all available regions with minimal maintenance. The app was responsive, easy to use, and had a low bug count post-launch.
</Outcomes>
