The Vita's original menu is a grid of app bubbles, and its homebrew lives in separate tools that each work differently. VitaOS brings everything into one place: games across RetroArch, PSP and Vita, films, music, photos, apps and a homebrew store.

## Designing for a 5-inch screen

Everything sits behind tabbed navigation that's quick to learn and works with the buttons or the touchscreen. Home puts what you played last first, with Quick Resume, the time, battery and weather on one screen.

## End to end

I designed and built it myself: the information architecture, the interface and motion, the sound and the code. It's free and open source, with releases on GitHub.
