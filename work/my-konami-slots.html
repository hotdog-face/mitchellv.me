My Konami Slots - App Art Direction

  • March 2019
  • Art Direction
  • Branding
  • UI/UX

During my time at Playstudios I got the privilege to Art Direct My Konami Slots. The app requirements were to make a Konami branded mobile casino app that feels like a high end casino with asian accents. The app was made through a partnership deal that allowed us to use Konami's slot game IP within the app that we produced. My Konami is now a top grossing casino genre app on the IOS and Android app store. Available on IOS, Android, Kindle and Facebook. Production art images range from final in-game assets to early concepts.
