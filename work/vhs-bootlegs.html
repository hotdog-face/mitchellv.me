VHS Bootlegs

Custom VHS Covers for modern films

  • July 2019
  • Graphic Design
  • Illustration
  • Branding

Some movies are just meant to be on VHS, so I've taken it upon myself to create my own custom VHS, from the box art, labels, to the previews on the tape before the film plays. This passion project is one that puts old materials to good use and showcases new films on a beloved old medium that technology has left behind. If you are also a collector of VHS tapes and would like to add some new films to your collection, I sell the digital files ready to print on my Etsy Shop. Thanks for the support and feel free to message me if you have questions, suggestions, or requests.
